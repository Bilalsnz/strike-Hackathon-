What the numbers mean

Estimated P&L is price movement × position units, adjusted for long or short direction. Leverage changes estimated margin, not dollar P&L for a fixed notional.

⚠

Liquidation is deliberately excluded

Real liquidation depends on maintenance-margin tiers, margin mode, fees and exchange rules. No liquidation price is fabricated in this version.
